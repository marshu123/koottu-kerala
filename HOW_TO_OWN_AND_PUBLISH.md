## 2. Publish It to the World for Free (Under Your Name)

You can launch this website online in less than 2 minutes using any of these **100% Free** options:

### Option A: Drag & Drop on Netlify (Easiest - 1 Minute)
1. Go to [https://app.netlify.com/drop](https://app.netlify.com/drop)
2. Drag and drop the folder:
   `C:\Users\marsh\.gemini\antigravity-ide\scratch\kerala-connect-web`
3. Netlify will instantly give you a free live public web link (e.g. `https://kerala-koottu-marsh.netlify.app`) that you can share with anyone on WhatsApp!

---

### Option B: Free GitHub Pages
1. Create a free GitHub account at [github.com](https://github.com).
2. Create a repository named `koottu` (or your preferred name).
3. Push the files from `C:\Users\marsh\.gemini\antigravity-ide\scratch\kerala-connect-web`.
4. Go to **Settings ➔ Pages** and select `main` branch.
5. Your website will be live at `https://<your-username>.github.io/koottu/` forever, with zero monthly costs!

---

### Option C: Get Your Own Custom Domain (.in or .org)
If you want a dedicated address like `koottu.in`, `keralasafehaven.in`, or `sahayi.org`:
1. Buy a `.in` domain on Namecheap or GoDaddy (usually ₹399/year).
2. In Netlify or GitHub Pages, click **"Add Custom Domain"** and enter your domain name.
