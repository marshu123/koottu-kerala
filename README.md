# Koottu (കൂട്ട്) — Kerala Community Safe Stay & Travel Companion Network

> **"ദൂരദേശങ്ങളിൽ നമ്മൾ ഒറ്റയ്ക്കല്ല"** *(You are never alone in far lands)*
>
> An initiative created by **Marshid** to protect, shelter, and guide job seekers, students, and exam candidates from Kerala traveling to Uttar Pradesh, Delhi, and across India.

---

## 🌐 Live Website (Available 24/7 Worldwide)

👉 **[https://marshu123.github.io/koottu-kerala/](https://marshu123.github.io/koottu-kerala/)**

Open it from any smartphone or computer anywhere in the world!

---

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **All 28 Indian States & Major Cities**:
   - Filter by Uttar Pradesh (Lucknow, Kanpur, Noida, Prayagraj, Varanasi), Delhi NCR, Maharashtra (Mumbai, Pune), Karnataka (Bengaluru), and more.
2. **Live GPS Location & Distance**:
   - Tap **"📍 Turn Location ON"** to find the closest Malayali hosts and get direct Google Maps directions.
3. **Living Companion — അഞ്ജലി (Anjali)**:
   - Floating interactive companion with comforting voice audio to calm nervous travelers.
4. **Host Voice Notes**:
   - Tap **"▶️ വോയ്സ് കേൾക്കൂ"** on any host card to hear a personal audio message welcoming you.
5. **Candidate SOS Travel Board**:
   - Post your travel details when you have a low budget or fear of unfamiliar places.
6. **Hindi-Malayalam Survival Toolkit**:
   - Phonetic pronunciation cards with audio to communicate with auto drivers and station porters.
7. **Emergency Contacts**:
   - NORKA Roots 24x7 Helpline (`1800 425 3939`), Railway RPF (`139`), UP Women Powerline (`1090`).
