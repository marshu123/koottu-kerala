Station Survival & Zero-Budget Tips

How to travel safely on less than ₹200

🚂 Railway Station AC Waiting Rooms (₹20 - ₹30)

Never spend ₹1,500 on shady station hotels. Major stations across India (Lucknow, New Delhi, Kanpur, Dadar, Chennai) have official IRCTC AC Lounges on Platform 1. Showing your valid train ticket allows you to rest, charge your phone, and use clean bathrooms for just ₹20–₹30 per hour.

🚖 Auto Tout Scam at Railway Exits

Private brokers will approach you inside train compartments saying "Hotel chahiye? Auto chahiye?". Firmly ignore them. Walk out to the official Pre-Paid Booth or use the Rapido / Uber auto app on your phone, or take the direct Metro.

🍱 Safe Budget Food & Gurudwaras

Look for IRCTC Jan Aahaar cafeterias at stations for ₹20 Janata Khana or nearby Gurudwaras (e.g. Naka Hindola in Lucknow, Bangla Sahib in Delhi) where free hot langar meals are served with utmost dignity and warmth to anyone in need.
